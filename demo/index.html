We know what we don't know, and where.

A dependency ledger under a real HAZOP and real P&IDs. The engineer proposes a change; every claim that rested on what changed is re-derived by a solver and comes back as a proof, a refutation with the rows it rests on, or a question addressed to a person.

Real PUBLIC RECORD

  • Seven P&ID sheets MM0011–MM0017 (Burns & McDonnell, Rev A, 2021)
  • HAZOP worksheets, three nodes, eight pages (Hoglin Engineering, Feb 2021)
  • All 35 recommendations and the 5×5 risk matrix
  • Design pressure 1,800 psig, ANSI 900 piping, 12,980 ton/day
  • The blanks on the drawings: "SET @ XXX PSIG" on both override controllers, blank set pressures on four relief valves

Source: Project Tundra NEPA record, DOE/NETL, Appendices D–E.

Filled in SYNTHETIC, LABELLED

  • SRS and cause-and-effect for the two shutdown functions the HAZOP names

  • Historian excursions bracketing the design rate
  • MOC-001: raised compressor discharge pressure
  • A one-page site procedure; IEC 61511 and CCPS clauses paraphrased with clause ids

The documents, as the engineer receives them

Seven sheets, eight worksheet pages, a recommendations list, a matrix, and a change request. The arrows between them live in people's heads.

What the engineer sees today

One document at a time. The LOPA credit is a cell in a worksheet; whether the layer it credits shares a transmitter with the control loop is a different drawing, and nobody re-opens it when a setpoint moves.

Three ways into the same ledger

One ledger under the documents. Pick a way in; the stage below switches to it. Every surface answers the same two questions for anything you click: what does this rest on, and what rests on it.

01

From an open LOPA row to a scoped QRA

The LOPA as a spreadsheet. A row the solver has refuted or left open carries a lit button; press it and the row becomes the structure a QRA would need: a fault tree with the shared transmitter drawn once, an event tree, an inputs ledger, and the list of what no document states. Never a risk number.

02

Interactive P&IDs

Seven real sheets. Every box is a claim with a sheet and zone behind it. Click one for what it rests on and what rests on it; commit MOC-001 and watch the obligation walk the drawings.

03

Smart saves that mitigate PHA fatigue

LOPA, SRS and the alarm databases as a grid the engineer already knows. Click any cell for what it rests on and what rests on it. Before a save, the ledger states what the edit touches: every finding that will be re-decided, and anything resting on an unanswered question. No re-reading the whole PHA to find out.
